5 FAQs

Quick answers about medical breast reduction, coverage and recovery. Still unsure? Call us at 646-630-9178.

Is medical breast reduction covered by insurance?

Many plans cover it when it is medically necessary. Coverage depends on your insurer, your symptoms and records of past treatment such as physical therapy or pain management. We review your plan and help prepare your request.

What symptoms can breast reduction relieve?

Patients commonly seek relief from chronic back, neck and shoulder pain, deep bra-strap grooves, rashes under the breasts, headaches, and trouble exercising comfortably.

How long is recovery after reduction mammoplasty?

Many patients return to desk work in one to two weeks and resume full activity after about four to six weeks. Your surgeon will give you a timeline based on your procedure and healing.

Will I have scars?

Yes, breast reduction leaves scars. Their pattern depends on the technique used, and they usually fade with time. Your surgeon will show you where incisions are placed during your consultation.
